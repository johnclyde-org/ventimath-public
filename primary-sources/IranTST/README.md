# Iran TST — Iranian IMO Team Selection Tests

## Contest Owner

**Iranian Mathematical Olympiad Committee** (Young Scholars Club /
باشگاه دانش‌پژوهان جوان). The team selection tests (آزمون انتخاب تیم
المپیاد ریاضی) pick Iran's IMO team from the gold medalists of the
national olympiad; a year runs up to three exams of two days each,
every sitting three problems in 4.5 hours.

## Contents

- `official/iran-tst-{YEAR}.pdf` — the official Persian papers for
  2011–2017, each covering the full year's selection exams. Files are
  named by Gregorian year; the papers themselves carry the Persian
  year (1390–1396):
