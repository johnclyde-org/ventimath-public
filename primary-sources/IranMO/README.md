# Iran MO — Iranian National Mathematical Olympiad

## Contest Owner

**Iranian Mathematical Olympiad Committee** (Young Scholars Club /
باشگاه دانش‌پژوهان جوان). The national olympiad runs in stages: a
multiple-choice first round each winter, a two-day written second round
in IMO format, third-stage exams at the summer training camp, and
finally the team selection tests (stage 4 — see `../IranTST/`).

## Contents

Official Persian papers, filenames as the committee publishes them
(years are Persian; add 621 for Gregorian):

- `round1/` — first round, 1363–1400 (1984–2021): problems and
  solutions for most years (`*-first_round_*`), answer keys or
  problems-only for some recent years (`*-M1*`). A few years also carry
  a preliminary paper (`*-M1-Mogh*`).
- `round2/` — second round, 1363–1400: problems and solutions
  (`*-second_round_*`, recent years `*-M2*`).
- `round3/` — third stage (summer camp): sparse. Files numbered
  `10/11/12` are the 10th–12th national olympiads (1372–1374, i.e.
  1993–1995; the papers credit the book "المپیاد ریاضی در ایران" by
  Ebadollah Mahmoodian as their source), with `-1/-2/-3` marking the
  several third-stage exams of an edition; plus 1398–1399 (2019–2020)
  by year. `-P`/`-S` suffixes are problems/solutions.
