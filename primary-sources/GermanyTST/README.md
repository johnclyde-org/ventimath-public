# Germany TST — Auswahlwettbewerb zur Internationalen Mathematik-Olympiade

## Contest Owner

**Bildung & Begabung gGmbH** (Bundesweite Mathematik-Wettbewerbe). The
Auswahlwettbewerb zur IMO (AIMO) selects Germany's IMO team through a
series of Auswahlklausuren beginning each winter; each Klausur is three
problems in 180 minutes.

## Contents

Official German papers, filenames as the organizer publishes them:

- `official/aufgaben_awb_{YY}.pdf` — the complete problem booklet for
  each selection cycle, 2000–2020 (`_00` through `_20`).
- `official/loes_awkl1_{YY}.pdf`, `loes_awkl2_{YY}.pdf` — solutions to
  the first and second Auswahlklausur, 2002–2012.
- `official/loes_awkl_{YY}.pdf` — combined solutions, 2013–2020.
- `official/{YEAR}_IMO_Auswahlklausuren_HP.pdf` and
  `..._Lsg_HP.pdf` — problems and solutions for 2021 and 2022.
